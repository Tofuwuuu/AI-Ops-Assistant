# Reporting a Bug

## What to include

When reporting a bug, please provide:

- Steps to reproduce
- Expected vs actual behavior
- Browser / OS / app version
- Screenshots or error messages (redact secrets)
- Approximate time the issue occurred (with timezone)

## Severity

- **Critical**: service down or data loss — escalate immediately
- **High**: major feature broken for many users
- **Medium**: workaround exists
- **Low**: cosmetic or edge-case issues

Our team triages bugs within one business day for high/critical reports.
