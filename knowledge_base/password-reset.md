# Password Reset

## How do I reset my password?

1. Go to **Account Settings** → **Security**.
2. Click **Forgot password?** or **Reset password**.
3. Enter the email associated with your account.
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 24 hours).
5. Choose a new password that is at least 12 characters and not reused elsewhere.

If you do not receive the email within a few minutes, check spam/junk folders or contact support with the email address you used to sign up.
