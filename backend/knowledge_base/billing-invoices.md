# Billing & Invoices

## Where can I find my invoice?

Invoices are available under **Billing** → **Invoices** in your account dashboard. You can download PDF copies for the last 24 months.

## How do I update my payment method?

1. Open **Billing** → **Payment methods**.
2. Add a new card or bank account.
3. Set it as the default method.
4. Optionally remove the old method after the next successful charge.

## Refunds

Refund requests for accidental upgrades are reviewed within 5 business days. Include your invoice number and reason when contacting billing support.
